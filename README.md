## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing online news content to understand patterns associated with misinformation. The system uses machine learning techniques to classify news articles as real or fake based on textual data.

---

## Problem Statement
The rapid spread of online news has made it difficult to verify the authenticity of information. Fake news can mislead users, influence opinions, and create confusion in society. Identifying and distinguishing fake news from real news is a challenging task in the digital age.
