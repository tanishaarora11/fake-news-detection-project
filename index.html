How it works

Three steps from paste to verdict.

1

Paste your article or headline

Copy any news article, headline, tweet, or social media post into the analysis box. The system accepts text of any length.

2

AI analyses language patterns

The text is cleaned, tokenised, and vectorised through TF-IDF. Logistic Regression trained on 72,000+ articles then predicts credibility.
